Send yourself deal and promo alerts as real Android notifications, instead of email.

One-time setup

This hub delivers notifications through ntfy, a free push service. No account needed.

  1. Install the ntfy app from the Play Store on your Pixel.
  2. In the app, tap + and subscribe to the topic below (keep it private — anyone who knows it can send to it).
  3. Come back here to compose alerts. They'll show up on your phone within seconds.
